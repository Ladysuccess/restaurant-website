A Brief History

Located along the PH - Aba Expressway, near Port Harcourt Liberation Stadium, Hotel Presidential stands as a symbol of elegance and prestige in Rivers State's capital. Less than an hour's drive from the international and domestic airports, the hotel offers the perfect balance of accessibility and luxury.

Centrally situated within the city's key business districts and shopping areas, it is the preferred choice for both leisure and business travelers. With 241 tastefully furnished rooms and 53 spacious suites including exclusive accommodations on the Executive Floors 5th & 6th Floor Wing 1 and 4th to 7th Floors Wing 2 with the added privilege of private check-in and check-out through our Executive Lounge. Every space at Hotel Presidential combines style, functionality and refined comfort
